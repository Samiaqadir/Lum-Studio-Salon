Key Compliance & Safety Checks

  • Zero External API Leakage: No OAuth tokens or live publish endpoints are contacted.
  • Clear Status Separation: Posts exist strictly as Draft (unapproved copy) or Scheduled (approved for internal salon calendar).
  • Persistence: Stored locally in data/store.json for staff review.
