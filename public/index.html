EVERYTHING YOU NEED TO KNOW

Frequently asked
questions.

Clear details, transparent policies, and thoughtful answers before your visit.

Do I need a consultation before booking Balayage or major colour? +

Yes! We require a quick, complimentary 20-minute colour consultation before any first-time balayage. It allows us to examine your current strand health, discuss your hair history, map out your goals, and formulate a custom plan so your appointment goes smoothly and safely.

What is your cancellation and rescheduling policy? +

To keep your chair reserved specifically for you with zero double-booking, we kindly ask for at least 24 hours’ notice if your schedule changes. Cancellations within 24 hours or missed visits may incur a policy charge.

How should I arrive for my appointment? +

Please arrive with dry hair in its natural texture (washed within 24–48 hours) without heavy styling products. This allows your stylist to analyze your natural fall, cowlicks, and curl patterns.

Can I choose a specific stylist or book first available? +

Absolutely. You can select your favorite stylist (Maya, Jules, or Imani) to match your aesthetic, or choose “First Available Stylist” to see the earliest open appointment on your preferred date.

Where is Luma Studio and how do I get there? +

We are conveniently located at 214 Bedford Avenue in Williamsburg, Brooklyn. We’re just a 4-minute walk from the Bedford Ave station on the L Train, or a short walk from the Nassau Ave stop on the G Train.
